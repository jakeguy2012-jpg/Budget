# Roadmap

Future enhancements to consider after the MVP is stable:

- Better AI-assisted categorization with local review and no required paid API.
- Receipt upload and attachment support.
- Recurring bill detection.
- Net worth dashboard.
- Investment account tracking as read-only balances/transactions only.
- Goal tracking.
- PWA/mobile install support.
- Multi-household support.
- Optional external access guides for Tailscale, Cloudflare Tunnel, or VPN.
- Integration with Actual Budget as an import/export target.
- Full Plaid implementation if desired later, limited to Link, Transactions, and Balance read-only products.
